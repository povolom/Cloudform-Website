About the project

Website & brand

Cloudform

The website for Cloudform, the game-development and early software company I co-founded and led as CEO.

Visit the Cloudform site View code

What it is

A multi-page website bringing together the company story, gaming division, and planned software direction of Cloudform: a home page, a section for each division (Ciryx for games, Altyx for software), Explore pages for About, News, Careers and Events, a store preview and a mobile menu.

The store and cart are a demo: no products are for sale and nothing is collected at checkout.

My role

As co-founder and CEO, I contributed to product direction, project planning, branding, and team coordination. I iterated the site across more than 40 versions; version 41 is the one online today.
